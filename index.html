How it works

  1. 1. Sign in with Google — then complete your profile (name, country, city).
  2. 2. Post an item — choose Give (you offer it) or Need (you ask for it). Max 2 posts per week per account, so the feed stays fair.
  3. 3. Connect — interested people see your country & city and contact you. Agree on pickup. No payment, ever.
  4. 4. Confirm — when the item changes hands, tap “Mark as given” (or “Mark as received”). The item leaves the available list.

House rules

  • Everything is free. No selling, no fees, no “delivery charge” tricks.
  • Post only items you really own / really need. One item per post.
  • Meet in a safe public place. Never share passwords or bank details.
  • Be kind: first come is not a rule — the giver chooses who gets it.
